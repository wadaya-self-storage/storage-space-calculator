# Moving checklist

Adapt this to the move. Confirm actual dates, access arrangements and handling requirements with the people responsible.

## Preparation

- Sort by room: keep, sell, donate, recycle, discard or store.
- Decide which items you need during the move and on the first night.
- Measure large items and the doorways, lifts and corridors they must pass through.
- Confirm the moving date, vehicle access, parking, lift bookings and building time restrictions.
- Ask the mover or storage provider about item restrictions, lifting, fragile goods and stacking.
- Gather strong boxes, suitable protective material, tape, a marker and an inventory list.

## Packing

- Give each box a unique label: B001, B002 and onward.
- Record its room, contents and any “open first” priority.
- Keep the same ID on the physical box and the inventory.
- Pack heavy items in manageable quantities. Protect fragile items and mark handling needs.
- Seal the box, then measure its outer length, width and height in centimetres.
- Keep a day-one bag separate: essentials, chargers and items you will need immediately.
- Keep identity documents, valuables, medicines and access keys with the person responsible.

## Moving day

- Keep your inventory and contact details accessible.
- Check box IDs as items leave and arrive.
- Keep priority items accessible for unloading.
- Record visible damage or missing items while the relevant people are present.
- Follow the mover's lifting and loading instructions.

## Arrival or storage

- Match received items against the list.
- Record where each box is placed.
- Keep any needed access route clear and discuss safe stacking with the provider.
- Review “open first” items before putting the rest away.
- Save the updated inventory privately for the next retrieval or move.
