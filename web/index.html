How the calculation works

Length × width × height × quantity ÷ 1,000,000 gives cubic metres when dimensions are in centimetres. One 60 × 40 × 40 cm box is 0.096 m³. Four are 0.384 m³.

Measure outer dimensions after packing. Irregular objects use the smallest rectangular envelope you can measure. Ask your mover or storage provider about doorways, stacking, access and handling requirements.

Open a Moving Kit CSV or the Wadaya planner’s box-inventory CSV. Each planner row is one box; fill in its dimensions first. Planner notes and decisions remain in your original file, and the calculator counts every imported row. Imported totals are recalculated.

CSV exports use the same schema as the Python library. Text beginning with a spreadsheet formula marker receives an apostrophe.
